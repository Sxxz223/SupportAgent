# Nano 70W power distribution

## Multiple ports change available power

Power is redistributed when multiple ports are used. With two ports, supported distributions include 45W plus 22.5W or 60W plus 5W. With all three ports, the listed distribution is 45W plus 7.5W plus 7.5W.

For a laptop that requires 65W or more, use a single USB-C port when full charging speed is needed. A slow-charging notice can appear when several ports are active because the laptop no longer receives the full single-port output.
