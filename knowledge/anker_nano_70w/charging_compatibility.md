# Nano 70W charging compatibility

## Reaching 70W

Use one USB-C port, confirm that the device accepts up to 70W input, and use a cable rated for at least 70W. Devices commonly reduce their requested power as the battery fills, so output does not remain at 70W continuously.

## Mac charging icon delay

When first connected, macOS may briefly show a plug icon while output ramps up. The icon can change to a lightning bolt after about one minute while charging continues normally.

## Low-current devices and protocols

For niche low-current devices such as some electric shavers or hearing aids, Anker recommends using the USB-A port alone. The official FAQ lists QC, PD, UFCS, and PPS support and notes limits for proprietary protocols.
