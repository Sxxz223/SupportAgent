# Liberty 4 NC pairing and reset

## Reset procedure

Place both earbuds in the charging case and keep the case open. Make sure both earbuds are connected to the case. Press and hold the charging-case button for about 10 seconds. The LEDs flashing white rapidly three times confirms a successful reset.

After reset, enable Bluetooth, find soundcore Liberty 4 NC, and pair it. If pairing still fails, try another phone or tablet to determine whether the problem follows the earbuds.
