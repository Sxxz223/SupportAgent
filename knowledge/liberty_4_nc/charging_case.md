# Liberty 4 NC charging-case checks

## Earbuds or case do not show charging contact

Check that both earbuds sit firmly in the charging case and that the case LEDs confirm contact. Make sure the charging case and earbuds have sufficient power. Inspect the earbud and case charging contacts, and clean the charging pins with a dry cloth if dirt or debris prevents contact.
