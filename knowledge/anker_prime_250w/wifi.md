# Prime 250W Wi-Fi and app support

## Wi-Fi connection

The Anker support center provides Anker app instructions for connecting this charger to Wi-Fi and reconnecting Wi-Fi after deletion. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity can affect app-managed display features. Use the current charger firmware and current Anker app before troubleshooting an app or Wi-Fi feature further.
