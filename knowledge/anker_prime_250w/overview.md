# Anker Prime Charger (250W, 6 Ports, GaNPrime)

## Product facts

Model A2345 provides six ports: four USB-C ports and two USB-A ports. Total output is up to 250W. The charger includes a 2.26-inch LCD display for charging feedback and supports personalized power management.
