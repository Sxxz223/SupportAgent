# Prime 250W charging power

## Single-port output

Only USB-C1 supports up to 140W. USB-C2, USB-C3, and USB-C4 support up to 100W each. USB-A1 and USB-A2 support up to 22.5W each. Total output across all six ports is up to 250W.

For a reported 140W issue, use USB-C1 and a cable rated for the required power. The connected device must support the corresponding input, and actual draw can vary with device compatibility and battery level.

## Slow or intermittent charging

Confirm the device is connected to the intended port and account for power being shared when multiple ports are active. Firmware versions can change power-distribution behavior. If the product stops working, overheats, or charges intermittently, use the official model-specific support path rather than assuming a display or indicator problem.
