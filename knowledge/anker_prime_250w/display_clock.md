# Prime 250W clock display support

## Clock screensaver does not appear

Check whether Wi-Fi or Bluetooth is connected and working. Confirm that the charger firmware and Anker app are current. Firmware v1.2.3.2 with Anker app 3.2.0 supports the clock screensaver over Bluetooth without Wi-Fi; earlier versions such as v1.2.1.8 require Wi-Fi.

Check the screen timeout setting, select Clock Screensaver, and allow the configured idle time to elapse. Confirm the screen was not manually turned off; toggling the clock display off and on may help. Check the configured clock schedule or time range. Setting its start earlier than the current time can make the clock appear immediately.
